# Changelog

Plain-language, dated log of feature changes to the ERP. Newest entries at

## 2026-07-23 — Sales, Invoice & Quotation Redesign

The biggest change to date: rebuilt the invoicing system so the ERP can
replace Zoho as the official GST invoicing tool, plus a brand-new
Quotation/Proforma Invoice workflow.

**Invoicing correctness fixes**
- Invoice numbering now continues your real Zoho sequence — the next
  invoice generated by the ERP is `DBI2026/27-00681`, picking up exactly
  where Zoho left off. Numbers are minted by the server only and can never
  be edited or duplicated.
- Fixed a bug where GST was calculated assuming the business was in Delhi —
  it's actually Uttar Pradesh. Inter-state vs intra-state GST (IGST vs
  CGST+SGST) is now calculated correctly from real state codes.
- Fixed a bug where a sale in January–March could be filed under the wrong
  financial year.
- Fixed a broken "Download PDF" button on the Invoices page (it pointed at
  a page that didn't exist). Also found and removed a second, hidden PDF
  page that had **no login check at all** — anyone with a browser session
  could have viewed invoice and customer GST data through it.

**Business entities (Settings → Business Profiles)**
- Digitalbluez, Techtenth, and Cash are now proper configurable profiles —
  each with its own name, address, GSTIN, logo, signature, stamp, and bank
  details.
- Techtenth and Cash invoices now correctly show as a "Bill of Supply" with
  no GST — no tax is charged or shown, matching how those two entities
  actually operate.

**Combined invoices**
- On the Sales Ledger page, you can now select several un-invoiced sales for
  the same customer and generate **one combined invoice** covering all of
  them — matching how a real invoice usually has more than one line item.

**Quotations & Proforma Invoices (new: Sidebar → Quotations)**
- Create a price quote or a proforma invoice for a customer before they've
  committed to buying.
- Each has its own numbering series (`QUO...`, `PI...`), completely separate
  from real tax invoices.
- When a customer accepts, click "Convert" on a line to sell that specific
  unit — it opens the normal Sell screen with the customer and price already
  filled in. You can convert some lines and leave others open.
- Download as PDF, with a clear "Not a Tax Invoice" notice.

**Customer GST lookup**
- On the customer forms, entering a GSTIN and clicking "Fetch" now
  auto-fills the legal name, address, and state.

**Emailing invoices/quotations**
- An "Email" button now sends the PDF straight to the customer.
- ⚠️ **Not live yet** — needs a one-time setup (see "Needs your action"
  below).

**Cleanup**
- Removed unused/dead code found during this work: an abandoned sales page,
  three leftover database functions from an old design, an unused database
  table, and a duplicate database rule that no longer served a purpose.

## 2026-07-22 — Employee-Facing Operations, Permissions & Backdating

- Added the full employee-facing operational system: Stock Intake, Sell,
  Service (Repair/Replacement/Return), Accessories — all with immediate,
  real-time inventory effect (no owner-approval gate).
- Added per-employee page access control (Settings → Users & Access) —
  the owner can create logins and choose exactly which tabs each employee
  can see, without needing developer help.
- Redesigned Settings into category-based navigation (Zoho-style).
- Added a pre-GST/post-GST toggle to the Sell form.
- Added backdating support to Stock Intake and Sell (for entering
  transactions that happened earlier than today).
- Fixed a bug where backdated sales weren't showing up in Reports' month/year
  filters.
- Cleaned up leaked test data and a batch of real bugs found while entering
  live purchase data (PO totals, asset numbering, SKU dropdowns, Fix SKU).
